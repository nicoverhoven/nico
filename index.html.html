Our Story

We don't sell cars.
We deliver obsessions.

VELOCE began in 2012 as a single bay in Beverly Hills, built on a stubborn idea: that buying an extraordinary car should feel as extraordinary as driving one. Today we operate across eighteen countries — yet every client still deals with one person, from first conversation to final key handover.

Our atelier sources only fully documented examples. Each car is inspected over ninety points, photographed in our studio, and delivered in a covered transporter with a dedicated specialist.

  • 24/7 Concierge

    A named specialist on call, in your timezone.

  • Global Delivery

    Enclosed transport and customs to 18 countries.

  • Bespoke Config

    Paint, leather, carbon — specified to your signature.

  • Provenance Warranty

    24-month cover and a full documented history file.
